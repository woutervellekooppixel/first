Brand vision

FIRST Rotterdam stands at the front door of the city, directly at Rotterdam Centraal. The brand speaks with the calm confidence of prime real estate: precise, architectural and welcoming.

Decision makers of (inter)national companies, advised by Cushman & Wakefield. Professional, design-aware and time-poor: clarity wins.

Logo — 1.1

Primary logo

The FIRST Rotterdam wordmark is a purpose-built typographic mark. The heavy capitals carry the confidence of the tower; the angled cut of the T points forward and upward.

Always reproduce the logo from the master artwork. Never redraw, reset or modify it. The full-color version in FIRST Slate is the default on light backgrounds.

Brand — FIRST Rotterdam
Logo — 1.2

Clearspace & minimum size

Give the logo room to lead. The exclusion zone on all sides equals the cap height of the letter F — marked as X. No text, images or graphic elements may enter this zone.

Below the minimum size, the ROTTERDAM baseline becomes illegible. Use the FIRST-only master artwork for very small applications such as favicons.

Color — 2.1

Core palette

FIRST Slate is drawn directly from the logo and is the backbone of the identity. Skyline Ink replaces pure black in all typography. Signal Orange is reserved for accents: highlights, arrows, calls to action — never for large surfaces.

Mist and Cloud keep layouts airy and architectural. When in doubt: more white, less color.

CMYK values are indicative — always proof against the printer's color profile before production.

04
Speak like the address: assured, precise, welcoming.

We don't shout about being first.

Voice profiles

Architectural authority

Decision makers want facts, floorplans and clarity — not vague promises. This tone is precise and structured: square metres, connections, certifications, terms. It positions FIRST as a professionally managed, premium address and builds immediate trust with tenants and their advisors.

The confident host

Where authority handles the logic, the host handles the feeling. It speaks to the pride of arrival: your name at the best address in Rotterdam, your team one step from Centraal. Warm, direct and generous — like a concierge who already knows your name.

Say

"Five floors available. One minute from Centraal. Zero compromises."

Don't say

"A unique, one-of-a-kind opportunity in a truly iconic landmark!!"

Imagery — 4.2

Photography direction

Three image families, one feel: cool daylight, architectural lines, honest people. Slight cool grade toward slate; never oversaturated, never staged stock-smiles.

Image — Architecture

The tower

Strong verticals, low vantage points looking up, glass and sky. Shot at golden hour or in crisp daylight.

Image — City

The city

Rotterdam in motion: Centraal, skyline, transit. Long exposures and elevated views emphasise connection.

Image — People

The tenants

Real professionals in real moments — arriving, meeting, focused. Natural light, candid framing, no posing.
