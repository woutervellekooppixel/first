01 Vision - 01

Not the loudest building - the best positioned one.

Brand vision

The brand speaks with the calm confidence of prime real estate: precise, architectural and welcoming. Every expression reinforces one promise - being first.

Positioning

First location, first impression, first choice. FIRST doesn't shout about being first; it simply is - and it shows, in every layout and every word.

Audience

Decision makers of (inter)national companies, advised by Cushman & Wakefield. Professional, design-aware and time-poor: clarity wins.

03 The cut - 03

The cut. Our sharpest asset.

Two gestures own Signal Orange. The cut - sliced from the T - anchors a corner, points the way or splits a surface. The point is the full stop behind every promise: one orange period that ends the headline.
